FaQ

Most Asked Questions

1. How old do I have to be to buy an Insurance?

You must be at least 18 years old to purchase an insurance policy. For minors, a guardian can register on their behalf.

2. What is ChildCare Insurance?

ChildCare Insurance provides health and life protection for children. It covers medical costs, emergencies and future savings.

3. Do you accept credit cards?

Yes, we accept all major credit and debit cards for payments. You can also choose secure online or bank transfer options.

4. How do Insurance plans work?

Insurance plans collect small premiums to cover big losses. You’re protected financially when unexpected events occur.

5. Who is eligible for the Future Insurance plan?

Anyone between 18 and 60 years of age can apply. It’s ideal for those planning long-term savings and security.

6. Why should I have Insurance?

Insurance protects you and your family from financial risks. It ensures peace of mind during life’s unpredictable moments.
