Services We Provide

Skin Diseases Detection

Upload or capture an image of your skin lesion and our deep learning system instantly scans it for signs of melanoma or other skin diseases.

Actinic Keratoses and Intraepithelial Carcinoma

These are precancerous lesions that can develop into skin cancer if untreated. Early detection is essential for prevention and effective treatment.

Benign Keratosis Lesions

Includes seborrheic keratoses and solar lentigines. These are usually non-cancerous but can resemble malignant lesions, making accurate AI analysis vital.

Basal Cell Carcinoma

A common type of skin cancer that grows slowly and rarely spreads. Detecting BCC early helps prevent tissue damage and scarring.

Melanoma

The most dangerous form of skin cancer. Our AI system focuses heavily on identifying melanoma at its earliest stage when treatment is most effective.

Melanocytic Nevi

Most moles are harmless but changes in size, color or shape may indicate risk. Our system tracks such changes for early alerts.
